Quit line

Gating works here even though the calibration does not: at >=0.9 it is 80.1% on 78.4% of tickets, 8.3pt above ungated. What ECE 0.213 costs you is the right to quote the confidence as a probability, not the right to threshold on it.

Auto slice: at Jev confidence ≥ 0.9, 80.1% accurate on 78.4% of traffic, which is +8.3pt against 71.8% ungated. That threshold is the strongest one still covering at least half the traffic, picked by rule from the measured gates.

Calibration: ECE 0.213 (max bin gap 0.39). ECE says whether the confidence can be read as a probability. It does not say whether gating helps: thresholding only needs the model to rank its own answers, so a badly calibrated task can still gate well. High ECE costs you the right to quote the number, not the right to threshold on it.
