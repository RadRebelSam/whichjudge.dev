Auto slice: at Jev confidence ≥ 0.9, 92.9% accurate on 84.4% of traffic, which is +7.3pt against 85.6% ungated. That threshold is the strongest one still covering at least half the traffic, picked by rule from the measured gates.

Calibration: ECE 0.096 (max bin gap 0.34). ECE says whether the confidence can be read as a probability. It does not say whether gating helps: thresholding only needs the model to rank its own answers, so a badly calibrated task can still gate well. High ECE costs you the right to quote the number, not the right to threshold on it.
