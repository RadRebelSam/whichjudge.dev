Calibration: ECE 0.075 (max bin gap 0.138). ECE says whether the confidence can be read as a probability. It does not say whether gating helps: thresholding only needs the model to rank its own answers, so a badly calibrated task can still gate well. High ECE costs you the right to quote the number, not the right to threshold on it.
