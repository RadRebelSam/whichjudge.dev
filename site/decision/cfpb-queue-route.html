At >=0.9: 91.2% on 77.4% of complaints, 8.4pt above ungated. ECE 0.100 means do not repeat the confidence to anyone as a probability; it does not stop you thresholding on it.

Auto slice: at Jev confidence ≥ 0.9, 91.2% accurate on 77.4% of traffic, which is +8.4pt against 82.8% ungated. That threshold is the strongest one still covering at least half the traffic, picked by rule from the measured gates.

Calibration: ECE 0.1 (max bin gap 0.435). ECE says whether the confidence can be read as a probability. It does not say whether gating helps: thresholding only needs the model to rank its own answers, so a badly calibrated task can still gate well. High ECE costs you the right to quote the number, not the right to threshold on it.
